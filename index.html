About us

We keep institutions connected to people who've left.

AlmaConnect is an alumni data and engagement company that helps universities, schools, foundations, nonprofits and companies find where their alumni and donors are now, monitor 400,000+ news and public-data sources for events in their lives, and run the networks that keep those communities connected.

We started in 2011 building alumni networks. Six years ago we moved into the harder half of the problem: finding people after they stop telling you where they are.
