Camera

Camera moves

Moves apply over the selected words' time span, or 3 s from the playhead.

Depth of field

Between these two distances from the camera the text is crisp. Nearer or further and it softens, so words blur in as they pass the lens and blur out as they fall behind.

Track

Pin words to something in the shot — a screen, a sign, a face — so they stay on it while the real camera moves. Mark the object at one frame (or let the editor find objects for you), then track.

Tracking detects the features inside your region and follows all of them, so a few going wrong cannot pull the text off. If it does drift, scrub to that moment, drag the mark back where it belongs (that writes a correction key) or re-mark the object and track again. No video? Tracks can also be keyed by hand.

Mask

Cover the person with a soft shape so words marked “Behind subject” pass behind them — the strongest depth cue there is.

This is a shape you place by hand, not automatic cut-out. A soft rounded shape over a person reads convincingly as text passes behind them. If the subject moves, add mask keys at a few times and the shape will follow.
