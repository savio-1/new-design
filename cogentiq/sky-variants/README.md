# Hero sky candidates

Seventeen ramps for the homepage gradient, generated by `gen-variants.py` from the
design-system rungs in `../index.html`. Everything but the ramp — saturation,
bloom, falloff — is identical across all seventeen, so the comparison is only ever
about colour. Eight travel through two or three colour families, nine through four or
five. `17-blue-red-orange` is the Figma stops (Orange/500, Red/500,
Blue/500) read bottom-up, the way this panel is built.

The homepage ships 17 (the Figma stops), baked into the `--sky-*` tokens in
`../index.html`. To change it, copy another ramp's stops out of
`gen-variants.py` into those two blocks -- the dark run and the light one.

Side-by-side: <https://claude.ai/code/artifact/9a99db46-a765-4924-84b9-20e8bbc5a166>

Regenerate after any change to the ramps:

    python3 gen-variants.py

Once one is picked, its stops go into the `--sky-*` tokens in `../index.html`
and this folder can go.
