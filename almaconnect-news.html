What is AlmaConnect News?

AlmaConnect News is a prospect monitoring platform for advancement and nonprofit fundraising teams. It handles the continuous side of prospect research: monitoring an institution’s alumni and donor database against 400,000+ news and public-data sources, verifying each match, and alerting gift officers when a prospect makes news, files with the SEC, changes jobs, or passes away.

Your institution’s name is not in the news about your alumni.

When one of your alumni is appointed to a board, sells a company or wins an award, the story is about their work. Your institution rarely gets a mention. So an alert set on your institution’s name returns coverage of you — not coverage of the people you fundraise from.

Searching by constituent name alone is no better. Teams managing 100,000 alumni have hundreds of duplicate names, and a keyword alert from Google Alerts cannot tell which John Smith graduated from your school — least of all once he has stopped updating LinkedIn.

1. You share your constituent database.

Names, your unique IDs, education and employment history. Alumni, parents, trustees, corporations, grant-making foundations — anything you want tracked. Upload a file or connect your CRM.

2. AlmaConnect News builds search terms for each person.

Name plus employer. Name plus city. Name plus graduation year. Name plus spouse. Every combination your data supports, generated automatically — the search you would run by hand for one prospect, run continuously for all of them.

3. Those terms run against 400,000+ sources, every day.

National, local, international and industry publications, plus obituaries and public financial filings. Each potential match is checked against the context of the article before it is scored.

4. Verified results reach the right person.

Results are graded by confidence, sorted into 11 categories, de-duplicated across outlets, and routed to the gift officer who owns that prospect — by email digest, in the platform, or written to your CRM.

Every result tells you who, what and why — before you click.

Each result carries the publication, the headline, the single sentence from the article that actually concerns your prospect, and the reason they were featured. Not the opening line of the story — the line about your person. Most of the time you can act on the card without opening the article.

National titles matter less than you would expect. Most news about alumni appears in local papers, regional business journals and trade press — so AlmaConnect News monitors those heavily, alongside international publications for alumni abroad.

Obituaries and public financial filings are tracked as distinct sources, not scraped from general news — so a death notice or an insider transaction reaches you even when no article follows.

Track Form 3 and Form 4 filings to catch insider transactions and identify company insiders you did not know you had. A wealth event in a filing often precedes the news coverage by weeks — and sometimes there is no coverage at all.

Chinese-language sources remain limited. Some publications sit behind paywalls — you will get the headline, the relevant sentence and the category, and usually a second outlet covering the same story. Archived results are searchable for 90 days. If a publication matters to your institution and we don’t track it, tell us during onboarding and we will check.
