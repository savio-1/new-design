    What is AlmaConnect for Institutions?

    AlmaConnect for Institutions is an alumni engagement platform for schools, colleges and universities. It gives alumni a searchable directory, jobs board, events, interest groups and mentoring on web and mobile; keeps profiles current automatically; and surfaces alumni achievements from 400,000+ news sources — supported by a dedicated account manager who helps run the programme.

    Most alumni portals are busy for a month, then quiet for years.

    Launch goes well. A few hundred people sign up, post a photo, and never return. The real community carries on in WhatsApp groups nobody owns, job referrals get lost in a scroll, and your directory ages quietly — nobody updates a college record after a promotion.

    The platform is rarely why it fails. Keeping a community alive is a full-time job, and most alumni offices are two people with a hundred other things to do.
