How Cogentiq helps your business

Cogentiq is Fractal’s enterprise agentic AI platform. It lets your teams build, run and govern AI agents on the systems you already run, so work that needs judgement gets done faster, with people still in control.

What Cogentiq does for your business

1

Puts agents to work on the systems you already run

Agents read your ERP, CRM, data platforms and documents in place, through secure connectors. Nothing is migrated or copied.

2

Gives every agent the same understanding of your business

One enterprise ontology holds your definitions, policies and data bindings, so every team and every agent works from the same meaning.

3

Takes agents from pilot to production, then reuses them

Connectors, context and tools are built once and shared, so each new use case starts from a working base instead of from scratch.

4

Keeps every agent governed, monitored and observable

Policy, guardrails and audit apply to every run, and you can see how each agent is performing, what it costs and why it decided what it did.

Governed, monitored and observable, by default

One control plane sits between every agent and your enterprise, so you can trust what agents do, see how they perform and explain every decision.

Govern

Control what every agent may do, before it runs.

  • Identity, SSO and role-based access
  • Policies and guardrails every agent inherits
  • Human approval for high-stakes steps
  • A full audit trail of every run

Monitor

Know how every agent is performing in production.

  • Runs, success rate and latency
  • Quality scores from continuous evaluations
  • Cost and usage per agent, team and workspace
  • Alerts when an agent drifts or is blocked

Observe

See exactly why an agent did what it did.

  • A trace of every step, tool call and hand-off
  • The sources behind every answer
  • Which policy allowed or blocked an action
  • Who approved, and when
