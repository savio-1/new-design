About Us

About
AlmaConnect

AlmaConnect is an alumni data and engagement company, grounded in a simple belief: institutions should never lose touch with the people who made them.

Founded in 2011, AlmaConnect began by building alumni networks — then moved into the harder half of the problem: finding people after they stop telling you where they are.

Today that work spans 500+ universities, schools, foundations, nonprofits and companies worldwide — from Harvard Medical School to Punahou School.

From finding where every alum works now, to surfacing the news worth acting on, to running the networks that keep communities connected — our work turns a static database back into living relationships.

News + Data Mine

Finding people others miss

01 / 02

Run News and Data Mine together.

When your alumni make news, the story is rarely about their school.

Research and advancement teams often run News and Data Mine together — a job change found in the news updates how we search for that person, and vice versa. About 60% of our customers use both.

A story about one of your alumni is usually about their company, their appointment or their deal — your institution’s name isn’t in it. So we search for the person, not your name, and read the article to confirm the match before it ever reaches you.
