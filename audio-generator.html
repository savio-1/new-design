Browser speech has no file output, so Readback records what your speakers play. Choose This tab and tick Share tab audio when your browser asks.

Download recording
Readback uses the SpeechSynthesis API, so it speaks with the voices already installed on this device — nothing is uploaded and no key is needed. Voice lists differ by browser and operating system: macOS and Windows ship dozens on-device, while Chrome adds network voices, marked in the list. Long scripts are split at sentence boundaries to sidestep the ~15-second utterance cut-off in Chromium.
