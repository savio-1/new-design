What is AlmaConnect News?

AlmaConnect News is a prospect monitoring platform for advancement and nonprofit fundraising teams. It handles the continuous side of prospect research: monitoring an institution's alumni and donor database against 400,000+ news and public-data sources, verifying each match, and alerting gift officers when a prospect makes news, files with the SEC, changes jobs, or passes away.

Prospect research teams at Bryant University, Willamette University, Rose-Hulman, San Diego State University, Marquette, the University of Liverpool and the World Wildlife Fund use AlmaConnect News.

Your institution's name is not in the news about your alumni.

When one of your alumni is appointed to a board, sells a company or wins an award, the story is about their work. Your institution rarely gets a mention. So an alert set on your institution's name returns coverage of you — not coverage of the people you fundraise from.

Searching by constituent name alone is no better. Prospect research teams managing 100,000 alumni have hundreds of duplicate names, and a keyword alert cannot tell which John Smith graduated from your school.

400,000+ sources, weighted towards the ones that actually mention your people.

National titles matter less than you would expect. Most news about alumni appears in local papers, regional business journals and trade press — so AlmaConnect News monitors those heavily, alongside international publications for alumni abroad. Obituaries and public financial filings are tracked as distinct sources, not scraped from general news.

SEC filings, as an add-on.

Track Form 3 and Form 4 filings to catch insider transactions and identify company insiders you did not know you had. A wealth event in a filing often precedes the news coverage by weeks — and sometimes there is no coverage at all.

What we don't cover.

Chinese-language sources remain limited. Some publications sit behind paywalls — you will get the headline, the relevant sentence and the category, and usually a second outlet covering the same story. Archived results are searchable for 90 days. If a publication matters to your institution and we don't track it, tell us during onboarding and we will check.

Frequently asked questions

What is AlmaConnect News?

AlmaConnect News is a prospect monitoring platform for advancement and nonprofit fundraising teams. It handles the continuous side of prospect research: monitoring an institution's alumni and donor database against 400,000+ news and public-data sources, verifying each match, and alerting gift officers when a prospect makes news, files with the SEC, changes jobs, or passes away.

How is AlmaConnect News different from Google Alerts?

AlmaConnect News differs from Google Alerts in coverage, verification and routing. Google Alerts matches keywords across a limited index and cannot tell which of your twenty-five John Smiths is yours. AlmaConnect News monitors 400,000+ sources, builds search terms from each constituent's employer, education and location, verifies every match against your constituent database, and routes results to the gift officer who owns the relationship.

How does AlmaConnect News find prospect news?

AlmaConnect News builds multiple search terms for every constituent — name plus employer, name plus city, name plus graduation year, name plus spouse — and runs them against 400,000+ news and public-data sources daily. Each potential match is checked against the article's context, graded high, mid or low confidence, and sorted into 11 categories before delivery.

Who is AlmaConnect News for?

AlmaConnect News is for advancement and fundraising teams at universities, independent schools, healthcare foundations and nonprofits. Prospect research and prospect development teams use it to discover major gift prospects; gift officers use it for stewardship and timing. Customers include Bryant University, Willamette University, Rose-Hulman, San Diego State University and the World Wildlife Fund.

Which CRMs does AlmaConnect News integrate with?

AlmaConnect News integrates with Blackbaud Raiser's Edge NXT via the SKY API, Salesforce — including Ascend, AffiniQuest/Advancement RM and Nonprofit Success Pack — and Ellucian. Results are written to the constituent record as notes with custom field mapping, on your approval. AlmaConnect News can also run with no CRM connection at all.

Can AlmaConnect News monitor our full database, not just a VIP list?

Yes. AlmaConnect News is priced by constituents monitored rather than by user seats, and the per-record cost falls as the number rises. Customers monitor between 300 and 300,000 constituents. Teams focused on prospect discovery typically monitor the full alumni base, because the prospects worth finding are the ones not yet in a portfolio.

Is AlmaConnect News GDPR compliant?

Yes. AlmaConnect News is GDPR compliant and certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the UK–US Data Bridge. AlmaConnect also holds SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001. Customer data is hosted on AWS, never shared with third parties, never used to contact your alumni, and deleted within 30 days of contract termination.
