Frequently asked questions

What exactly is Alaro?

Alaro is an AI-powered smart camera that understands what's happening around your space. It can recognise people, pets, objects and activities, helping you stay connected to the moments that matter.

Can I get a free trial?

Every Alaro comes with free returns. Live with it at home, and if it isn't right for your space, send it back.

Do you have a monthly plan?

You don't need one. Live view, smart alerts, moments and Ask Alaro all come with the camera, with no monthly fee.

Do you have discounts for students and educators?

Write to our support team with your institution ID and we'll tell you about any current education offers.

What forms of payment do you accept?

UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking and EMI on eligible cards.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. If you add an optional plan, cancel it any time from the Alaro app under Settings › Plan. Your camera keeps working and your moments stay yours.
