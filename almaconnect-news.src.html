    What is AlmaConnect News?

    AlmaConnect News is a prospect monitoring platform for advancement and nonprofit fundraising teams. It handles the continuous side of prospect research: monitoring an institution’s alumni and donor database against 400,000+ news and public-data sources, verifying each match, and alerting gift officers when a prospect makes news, files with the SEC, changes jobs, or passes away.

    Your institution’s name is not in the news about your alumni.

    When an alum joins a board, sells a company or wins an award, the story is about their work, not your institution. An alert on your own name finds coverage of you — not of the people you fundraise from.

    Searching by name alone is no better. Manage 100,000 alumni and you have hundreds of duplicates — Google Alerts cannot tell which John Smith is yours, least of all once he stops updating LinkedIn.

    Mid-conversion, under a change freeze, or facing a six-month security review? AlmaConnect News can run with no connection to your database at all. Add an integration later. Princeton started this way.

    400,000+ sources, weighted towards the ones that actually mention your people.

    Most alumni news appears in local papers, regional business journals and trade press, not national titles. That is where AlmaConnect News looks hardest, plus international titles for alumni abroad.

    Where the news actually is

    Obituaries and public filings are tracked as their own sources, not scraped from general news — so a death notice or an insider transaction reaches you even when no article follows.

    SEC filings, as an add-on

    Form 3 and Form 4 filings catch insider transactions and surface company insiders you did not know you had. A filing often precedes coverage by weeks — sometimes there is none at all.

    What we don’t cover

    Chinese-language sources remain limited. Behind a paywall you still get the headline, the relevant sentence and the category, usually with a second outlet on the same story. Results stay searchable for 90 days.
