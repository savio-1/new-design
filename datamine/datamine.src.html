    What is AlmaConnect Data Mine?

    AlmaConnect Data Mine is an alumni employment data platform for advancement services and prospect research teams. It identifies everyone who lists an institution on their public professional profile, matches them to that institution’s constituent records with a unique ID, and delivers verified job changes, promotions and employer updates on a weekly, monthly or quarterly cycle — into a searchable directory or straight into Raiser’s Edge NXT or Salesforce.

    A constituent record is only as current as the day someone last touched it.

    Alumni change employer, title, city and surname without telling you. Nobody updates a university record after a promotion. So the employment data your gift officers rely on when they plan a trip, build a corporate list, or decide who to call is, quietly, years out of date — and there is no way to tell which records are stale by looking at them.

    The usual fix is a data file, once a quarter, matched by hand. It closes the gap for a week and reopens it for eleven.

    Where the data comes from, and what that means.

    Data Mine is built from public, self-reported professional profiles. That has two consequences worth knowing before you buy.

    Not everyone who lists you is your alumnus

    Someone who took a two-week executive course may list your institution and not be your alumnus — you can remove them.

    Senior people stop updating

    Senior people often stop maintaining their profiles altogether, which is one reason customers run Data Mine alongside AlmaConnect News: news coverage catches the appointments that never make it onto a profile.

    We report employers as people report them

    We do not currently roll subsidiaries up to a parent company — if you need Collins Aerospace and its legacy entities treated as one, that mapping happens on your side today.
