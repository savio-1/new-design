What is AlmaConnect for Corporates?

AlmaConnect for Corporates is a corporate alumni network platform for HR, talent and employer brand teams. It gives former employees a branded community with a directory, jobs board, events and interest groups; keeps their profiles current automatically; and monitors 400,000+ news sources so you know when an alum moves into a role that matters to your business.

On someone’s last day, you delete the only way you had of reaching them.

Their account is deactivated, their email bounces, and a person who spent four years learning how your company works becomes someone you’ll pay an agency to find again. Meanwhile they join a LinkedIn group nobody moderates, or a WhatsApp thread that fades within a year.

Most companies know this is a waste. What stops them is that maintaining a community is real work, and nobody owns it.

Sign-in

Google, LinkedIn or personal email

Former employees use an account they already have, not the work email they lost on their last day.

What we won’t promise.

Most alumni engage by email, not in the app

The app is included and the active members use it, but email is what brings people back. Anyone selling you an alumni programme on app downloads is measuring the wrong thing.

Profile updates are periodic, not real time

Profile updates from public professional data refresh periodically rather than in real time, so a job change can take a few weeks to appear.

A network needs something from you

It needs a signal that the company is still there. We can produce the content, but a programme with no involvement from the company goes quiet, whoever builds it.
