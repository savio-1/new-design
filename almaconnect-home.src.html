Everything you know about your alumni was true on the day you recorded it.

Graduates change jobs, cities, surnames and companies. Senior people stop updating LinkedIn. Constituent records go quiet for years — and by the time you learn someone sold their company, joined a board, or lost a parent, the moment to reach out has passed.

Most institutions close this gap with Google Alerts, a spreadsheet, and whoever happens to notice something. That finds a fraction of what’s out there, on no particular schedule, and puts it in one person’s inbox.

We keep institutions connected
to people who’ve left.
