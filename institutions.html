    What is AlmaConnect for Institutions?

    AlmaConnect for Institutions is an alumni engagement platform for schools, colleges and universities. It gives alumni a searchable directory, jobs board, events, interest groups and mentoring on web and mobile; keeps profiles current automatically; and surfaces alumni achievements from 400,000+ news sources, supported by a dedicated account manager who helps run the programme.

    Most alumni portals are busy for a month, then quiet for years.

    Launch goes well. A few hundred people sign up, post a photo, and never return. The real community carries on in WhatsApp groups nobody owns, job referrals get lost in a scroll, and your directory ages quietly, because nobody updates a college record after a promotion.

    The platform is rarely why it fails. Keeping a community alive is a full-time job, and most alumni offices are two people with a hundred other things to do.

    What we need from you, and what we can’t do.

    A network needs a pulse from the institution: a few minutes most days, and someone who will approve members and say yes to ideas.

    We can’t be the voice of your institution

    We can produce the content and run the events. But a network with no signal from the college goes quiet, whoever built it.

    Profile updates are periodic, not real time

    Profiles refresh from public professional data periodically, so a job change may take a few weeks to appear in the directory.

    Size changes how a network behaves

    A few hundred alumni behave differently from fifty thousand. We will tell you honestly which of our institutions looks most like you before you sign anything.
