P Perspective 3D text for video
How it works
  1. Add a video, or just pick a background colour.
  2. Add words and drag them into place in the 3D layout — nearer words sit closer to the camera.
  3. Choose a camera move. The camera pulls back and reveals the words one by one.
Video
MP4, MOV, WebM… or drop a file onto the preview. You can also work without a video on a plain colour.
Video in the 3D scene

Tip: with the video at 150–200 % the camera can pull back a long way and the footage still fills the frame. The Camera Reveal template sets this up for you.
